# Code Quest parents' catch up

**When:** Sunday 16 August 2026, 68 minutes
**Where:** virtual, Google Meet
**Present:** Bolaji, Gbenga, Grandma, Funmi, Lina. Ayo's household on the Calvary account
**Recorded:** 16 August 2026, from the meeting transcript and notes

The first conversation where the family was asked whether Code Quest could be something they do together. The answer was yes.

---

## What was asked

Two things, plus the question underneath both.

1. Another Code Quest with the family's own kids. How soon, and what does it look like
2. Whether it could go beyond the family
3. Underneath both: could this be something we do together, in whatever capacity

## What was agreed

- **Code Quest 3 is happening.** Consensus, immediately and without hesitation
- **Own kids first, outside families later.** Gbenga's position, accepted: there is still work to do on what the thing actually is before testing it on strangers. Do not rebrand or expand before there are milestones and outcomes to point at
- **A syllabus, not another single heavy day.** Code Quest 2 was too much work to repeat in a fortnight. A defined pathway spreads the load and gives the kids somewhere to go
- **Not everyone has to be at every session.** This is what makes co-delivery possible for people with jobs and children
- **Sessions of 45 to 60 minutes.** Weekly is too much
- **Every session recorded.** For the log, and so it can go on the platform later
- **A summit at the end.** Smaller milestones divided among whoever is available, building to one larger event where everyone judges
- **Everyone records their own thinking** as voice notes or written notes, to be gathered and worked through together rather than lost in WhatsApp
- **Date: tentatively Saturday 29 August**, to be confirmed by chat

## The ideas the room brought

Recorded because the whole point of asking was to get these.

**Lina.** The kids should be the face of the programme by age range, with their own voices guiding younger children through the app. Emma for the five to sevens. Also: this is a learning platform, and one of the real skills is giving and receiving feedback. Also: keep the next session creative and technical, because that is what excites them, and feed the life skills in underneath without announcing them.

**Funmi.** The next one should be individual projects rather than teams, so every child presents alone and can be seen on their own terms. And judge them on presentation and the soft skills, with the technical work at the bottom of the ranking rather than the top.

**Gbenga.** Work backwards from outcomes. What question does the next event answer? Build a milestone plan where each stage proves something specific, so a decision like rebranding happens when there is evidence for it. Also: a hosting party in Chappaqua as the template for a home run event. Also: the app matters because it gives the kids autonomy to practise on their own between physical sessions.

**Grandma.** Take each child's temperament into account when giving feedback. Praise and challenge have to be fitted to the child. Also, her observation of the day: when they were bonding as cousins they were relaxed and jumping about, and the moment the presentations started every one of them was serious. Also her question: would people outside this family even know what Code Quest means.
