# 0001 · The name and what the thing is

**Date:** August 2026
**Status:** open, actively contested
**Decided by:** nobody yet

## The question

Is this a coding product for children, or a publishing platform for young makers?

## Positions on record

**Code Quest, as built.** Concrete. A parent knows what they are buying. Supported by category research: demand exists for kids coding, concrete language beats abstract, and the soft-skills transformation works better as a surprise than as a promise. Risk: competes in a crowded lane.

**Creator positioning, from Mayowa's debrief, 12 August 2026.** The unusual investment in the product is not curriculum, it is making a child's work safe enough to publish to the real web. Therefore coding is the first medium and building is the identity. The hero moment is publication. Risk: vaguer at the point of sale.

**The test both sides accept:** if an eleven year old girl who wants to be a makeup creator cannot see herself in it, it has gone too coding-niche.

## Where it stands

Both versions exist and are live side by side at `/codequest/positioning/`. The creator variant deliberately carries no name and has a switcher for Tekton, Plot, BuiltBy and a plain TBD.

Naming research found the plain-English authorship names are largely taken. `imadeit.ai` is a live kids business education company, which closes that route. `builtby.kids` is already owned and is the strongest unused asset, because with the .kids domain the address completes the sentence.

## Not decided

Everything. This file exists so that whoever decides can see what was considered.
