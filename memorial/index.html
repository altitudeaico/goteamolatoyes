Daddy · Shepherd · Man of God
10 September 2020 — 10 September 2026
His story
A life of faith and service

A life that pointed
people to Jesus.

Daddy gave his life to the work of God — in the pulpit, behind the guitar, at the lectern and with hands raised in praise. Six years on, we remember with love, and we give thanks for a life poured out in service.

“It pays to serve Jesus.”
01

He
Worshipped.

His faith was not simply something he spoke about. It was something he lived. Hands raised. Heart open. Eyes fixed on Jesus.

02

He
Preached.

He used his voice to share the message that shaped his life: a life given to Christ is never wasted. The truth he taught still speaks.

03

He
Served.

Whether speaking, teaching or simply being present, service was woven into who he was. Pastor to many. Daddy to us.

04

He
Sang.

There was music in his ministry too. A guitar in his hands, worship in his heart, and a song that continues through the lives he touched.

Six years on

We still remember.

We still miss the sound of his voice.
We still tell his stories.
We still carry what he taught us.
We still see the fingerprints of his life.
And the message hasn't changed.
It pays to serve Jesus.
