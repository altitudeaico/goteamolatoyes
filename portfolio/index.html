Who decides what

Governance

The holding layer

The family council

One seat per house. Four seats. Meets quarterly. It does not run initiatives — it approves new ones into the portfolio, appoints each CEO, and holds the reserved matters below. Nothing else.

The initiative layer

One CEO each, with real authority

Not co-CEOs, not a committee. One accountable driver who decides everything not on the reserved list, and informs the council. Proposed for Code Quest: Bolaji, confirmed by the council. The precedent is the point — whoever brings the next initiative gets the same deal.

The veto list · kept short on purpose

Reserved matters

  • Outside money or debt — taking investment, loans, or giving guarantees
  • Selling or licensing anything the portfolio owns
  • The family name and the kids' images — how both are used, anywhere
  • The children — anything involving them beyond what each parent individually consents to
  • Distributions versus reinvestment
  • Removing a CEO

Everything not on this list, the CEO decides.

Decided now, while it's hypothetical

Reinvest first

Profits compound in the treasury until the council votes a distribution. Agreed out loud early, because “when do we see money” is a far uglier conversation once there is money.

The clause that makes it a portfolio

The standing invitation

Any house may propose an initiative to the council with a one-pager and a named driver, and receives the same structure Code Quest is taking: commons for all, a driver pool for them, a CEO seat confirmed by the council. Code Quest is not the exception. It is the template, going first.
