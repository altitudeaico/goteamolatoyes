How to give a cut instruction
Scrub to the moment, tap Copy timecode, and send instructions in chat like:
C01 keep 0:04.2 to 0:11.8, then C03 from 0:02 to the end, hard cut, 9:16 for the feed
Half speed helps for precise moments. Nothing is cut until you say cut.
