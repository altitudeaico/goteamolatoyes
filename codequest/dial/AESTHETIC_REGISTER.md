# Straddling it
## Keep the transformation. Lose the medieval.

The problem is not fantasy. Fantasy is what makes a nine year old care. The problem is the **costume the fantasy is wearing**, which is currently swords, guilds, crests, gothic type and parchment. That reads as a particular subculture, and it is a subculture most eleven year old girls, and plenty of boys, quietly do not want to be seen in.

The test is not "would a kid enjoy this". It is **"would a kid show this to the friend whose opinion they care about most."** Medieval fails that test. Transformation does not.

---

## What is actually causing it

Named honestly, so we cut the right things:

| Element | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Swords, shields, armour, crests | **Cut.** This is the whole problem in five objects |
| Guild, realm, citadel, forge, waystone | **Cut.** Renaissance fair vocabulary |
| Gothic blackletter type, gold on parchment | **Cut.** The single strongest nerd signal in the current design |
| Torches, scrolls, runes, sealed doors | **Cut** |
| "Quest", "Quester" | **Weak.** Fine as a verb, dated as an identity. Nobody wants to be called a Quester at school |
| Transformation, the reveal, becoming | **Keep.** This is the good stuff and it is not medieval at all |
| Belts, ranks earned by capability | **Keep.** Dojo culture is cool. Martial arts is not nerdy, it is respected |
| "Mogul" | **Keep.** Aspirational, current, and it comes from music and business rather than fantasy |
| Ceremony around a debut | **Keep and amplify** |

Notice that the two things you invented that are strongest, the transformation and Mogul at the top of the ladder, are both already outside the medieval register. The medieval bits are the decoration, not the idea.

## The register that keeps the magic

Everything a nine to fourteen year old currently finds aspirational shares one structure, and none of it is nerdy:

**You have a name. You have a look. You have people. And there is a moment when you drop something and the world sees it.**

That is a musician. A footballer. A streetwear label. A creator. A racing driver. Every one of those is heightened, mythic and cinematic, and not one of them is medieval.

- **Music:** the artist name, the cover art, the drop, the first time you hear yourself somewhere unexpected
- **Football:** the shirt with your name on it, the tunnel, the debut, the badge
- **Streetwear:** the label, the mark, the release, people wearing your thing
- **Motorsport:** the livery, the number, the garage, the flag
- **Creators:** the handle, the aesthetic, the post that travels

These read as aspirational to boys and girls equally, which the sword does not. They are also all about **authorship and release**, which means the register happens to agree with Mayowa's positioning without anyone having to compromise.

## Where the dial sits

**Fantasy in the film and the moment. Reality in the world and the words.**

- **The transformation films: turn the fantasy up.** Slow motion, scale, light, a look that assembles, a real face. This is where kids get their goosebumps and it is worth every penny. Just change what is assembling: not plate armour, but a look the child chose, in their colours, with their mark.
- **The environments: ground them.** A studio, a stage, a rooftop, a garage, a city at night. Heightened and cinematic, but recognisably this world. The moment a citadel appears on a mountain, you have lost the eleven year old.
- **The typography: this matters more than people think.** Gothic blackletter is doing enormous damage. Move to something confident and modern with weight, the way a sports or music brand sets type. The current app already did this correctly with Nunito.
- **The interface: no fantasy at all.** Already settled and it still holds.

## The version of the promise that survives both tests

> **You make something. You put your name on it. The world gets to see it.**
>
> And the day it first goes live, we make a film about you.

There is the fantasy, and it is entirely real. The becoming is a debut, not a coronation.

---

## The one line to hold on to

Sword: nerdy.
Shirt with your name on the back: not nerdy.

Both are about being chosen and becoming someone. Only one of them can be shown to your mates.
