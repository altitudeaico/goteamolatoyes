Built for parents to trust

Safety by design

Parents hold the accountKids are profiles with a PIN. No child emails, no child passwords.
No ads to childrenEver. Subscription is the only model.
Human approvalNothing a child makes goes public unreviewed.
Visibility is per child, per parentA child's work can be famous. The child is exactly as visible as their parent chooses.

How it sustains

Simple, honest money

I
The event ticketPriced as an experience. Parents attend the showcase on general admission.
II
The merch stallThe child's own character, printed: poster, card, shirt.
III
The subscriptionConverted on the day, ticket credited against month one. Kids keep 100% of anything their builds ever earn.

Infrastructure costs are pennies by design. The cost of this business is coaches and venues, never servers.
