The order of work

Build sequence

1
Web marketplace

The gallery, seeded with the eight builds that already exist. Share links and referral codes work in URLs today.

2
Self-serve publishing

Drag a folder onto a secret link. Pending until approved. No accounts, no passwords, no children's-data problem yet.

3
The app as a wrapper

Once the web loop is proven. The app's real jobs: push, cohort presence, and App Store legitimacy in a parent's eyes.

4
Money features

Coins are a spreadsheet until that hurts. Ads and revenue share stay parked behind the Mogul tier decision.

Prove this first

The whole growth model rests on one thing: does the referral loop close? A kid shares, a friend plays, the attribution survives, the friend converts. Spike it on the web version before the app exists — if the loop doesn't close, nothing downstream matters.
