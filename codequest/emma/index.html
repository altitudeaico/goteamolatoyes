Play it

Rainbow Sprinkle Dash

Fly through the sky, eat the rainbow sprinkles and pop the bubbles. Every sprinkle you eat makes you go faster, and the music speeds up with you. You cannot lose. You just get faster.

Rainbow Sprinkle Dash Works on a phone, a tablet or a computer Start playing

If you have never played

How to play

1
Pick who you want to be

A unicorn, one of two cats, or Anna.

2
Hold down to fly up

Touch the screen and hold. Let go and you float back down. On a computer, hold the space bar.

3
Eat the sprinkles

Each one is a point, and each one makes you a bit faster.

4
Pop the bubbles

Worth five. They wobble, so you have to aim.

What is good about it

Worth knowing

Nobody loses. There is no game over. If you hit the top or the bottom you bounce gently and keep going. For a game made by the youngest person in the room, that is a real design decision, and a kind one.

The music is not a recording. Every note is made by the game while you play, and the tune speeds up as you get faster. Nothing is downloaded.

It is one file. No pictures, no sound files, nothing loaded from anywhere else. Everything you see is drawn as the game runs.
