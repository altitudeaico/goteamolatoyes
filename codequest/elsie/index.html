Three ways to play

The modes

Master

The city runs at you. Three lanes, trains to dodge and colour gates to pass. Hit one train and you are out.

Side on. Tap to jump and flip over spikes, holes and low ceilings.

3

She paints the line. Make your own character.

What is good about it

Worth knowing

It is three games, not one. Most people build a single mode and stop. Master and Easy are genuinely different games with different controls, and Draw is not a game at all — it is a tool for making your own character.

Easy exists on purpose. Master ends the moment you touch a train. Rather than making the game gentler for everyone, she built a separate mode for people who want to play without losing.

The music is made as you play. Every note is generated by the game rather than loaded from a file.

It has a studio name. Butterflii Art Studio. She did not just make a game, she made a label to put it out under.
