The guardrail · non-negotiable

The reference brand took public backlash for wrapping its marketing around a child influencer. Our line: the kid creates, the parent approves, the platform amplifies only what's opted in. Kids' work can be famous; kids themselves stay exactly as visible as their parents choose. Done right, this is a differentiator we market to parents directly.
