How this works

This is a small web app. It isn't in an app store and you don't install it. Everything goes into a database, which is just a very organised list that lives on the internet. That's why it shows up on everyone else's phone straight away, and why it's still here next week.

If you record a voice note, your phone writes down what you say as you say it. It gets things wrong sometimes, so you can correct it before you save. We keep both, the recording and the words.
