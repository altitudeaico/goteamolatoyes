What the judge said

The reckoning

They gave credit to each other. Nobody took credit for what somebody else did. On Pixel Power's teamwork
Showing the avatar, then themselves, then back again — that made it comfortable for people who are not in that world. On the music video
They said they tried nine and used five. Being able to look at your own work and say what actually happened is worth something. On honest self-assessment

The lesson that came out of the day, more than any other: building the thing is the easier half. Four of the ten marks were for explaining it, and that is where the two teams separated.
