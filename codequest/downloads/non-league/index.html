How to open it after you download it

  1. Find the file in your Downloads folder. It is called non-league-game.html.
  2. Double click it.
  3. It opens in your web browser and you can play.

The whole game is inside that one file, so it still works with the wifi off. You can email it to someone or put it on a memory stick and it will work on their computer too.

On a phone or tablet

Phones do not really have a Downloads folder you can dig around in, so downloading works best on a laptop. On a phone, use the play link above instead.
