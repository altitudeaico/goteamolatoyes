The decrees

  1. Three builds to each house. You rank them yourselves — best, second, third.
  2. One may be something you forged before today. The other two are made here, from nothing.
  3. A different speaker for each build. Everyone stands up once.
  4. Grandma is the judge. Her word settles it.
  5. Ezekiel arbitrates. Eldest in the room. When a call must be made, he makes it.
  6. Summon every tool you can. Music, avatars, graphics, video, slides. There is coin in it.
  7. When tools down is called, tools down. The final hour belongs to the telling, not the making.
